## 🌟 Executive Overview & Problem Statement

Career breaks are common due to maternity, childcare, caregiving, relocation, or personal circumstances. A career break does **not** erase a professional's foundational capability—yet traditional hiring systems only communicate *historical experience*, leaving employers with uncertainty:
- *How current are the candidate's technical skills?*
- *Have they worked with newer versions/frameworks (e.g. Java 17+, Spring Boot 3)?*
- *Can the candidate solve real-world production tasks today?*
- *What verifiable evidence exists beyond the historical resume?*

**SkillBridge** solves this evidence gap through a candidate-controlled evidence pipeline:
$$\text{Past Experience} \longrightarrow \text{Target Role} \longrightarrow \text{Skill Gap Diagnostics} \longrightarrow \text{Practical Evidence} \longrightarrow \text{Candidate-Controlled Profile} \longrightarrow \text{Employer Review}$$
